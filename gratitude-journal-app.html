About the Gratitude app

What can I do in the Gratitude app?

You can write private gratitude journal entries with guided prompts, create and listen to positive affirmations, build vision boards, and read daily motivation in Daily Zen.

Is the Gratitude app free?

Gratitude is free to download and includes optional in-app purchases for Pro features. You can explore the app before deciding whether Pro is right for you.

Is Gratitude available on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Gratitude is available from the Apple App Store for iPhone and iPad and from Google Play for Android devices.

Are my gratitude journal entries private?

Journal entries and app data are stored locally on your device by default. Optional backups use your private Google Drive or iCloud space; see the privacy policy for details.
