Why gratitude

  1. Notice

    See what is already supporting you.

    A warm drink. A kind message. Two quiet minutes. Gratitude starts by letting an ordinary moment count.

  2. Remember

    Return to the words you want to keep.

    Writing it down gives the moment somewhere to stay. Reading it again can bring the good closer on a day when it feels harder to find.

  3. Return

    Practice a gentler point of view.

    Each entry is a small pause. The practice makes room for difficulty and appreciation to exist in the same day.

  4. Connect

    Hold appreciation close, then pass it on.

    A private reflection can become a thank-you. What begins inside you can turn into a moment of connection.
